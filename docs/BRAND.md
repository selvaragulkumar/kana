# Brand

kana is powered by [ZAG](https://withzag.com). The app shows "powered by zag" under the panel footer and at the end of Setup, with the drawn ZAG wordmark (`app/Resources/Brand/zag-logo-white.png`, 19 pt tall, the brand minimum).

- The kana mark (`design/kana-mark.svg`, `design/kana-app-icon.svg`) and the ZAG wordmark are trademarks. The Apache 2.0 licence covers the code, not these marks (Apache 2.0 §6).
- Forks may keep the credit line or remove it, but must not present a modified build as kana or as a ZAG product.
- The ZAG wordmark is never re-typed as text and never recoloured; the white rendition is for dark surfaces, the blue one (`docs/images/zag-logo.png`) for light ones.
